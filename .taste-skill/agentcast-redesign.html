How a link becomes a video

Understand

It reads your site first.

TinyFish Fetch reads the landing page and docs. AgentCast writes a product brief and a creative brief: who the video is for and what it has to prove.

Explore

Agents walk the product.

Up to three TinyFish agents try flows side by side. Every flow they report is rehearsed in a local Chrome before you see it, and a judge picks the ones worth showing.

Record

Filmed at 60 fps, for real.

A Playwright recorder replays each approved flow in a local Chromium. When a button moves, its locator heals; when a page stalls, the take is shot again.

Direct

A coding agent cuts it.

Claude Code writes the script, times every cut to the voice and checks the edit with a motion lint. Ask for a change in plain words and it revises.

Render

Every size, every language.

HyperFrames renders 16:9 and 9:16 in English and Hindi, with captions that light up word by word as the voice speaks them.

Before you paste a link

Do I need an API key for the AI?

No. The writing and directing run through the Claude Code or Codex CLI you’re already logged into. The only keys are for TinyFish, which explores, and ElevenLabs, which speaks.

Which sites can it record?

Sites you own or are allowed to demo. AgentCast doesn’t solve CAPTCHAs and won’t try to get past one.

What about pages behind a login?

Sign in once. A normal Chrome window opens on the worker’s machine, you log in, then press “I’m signed in”. The recorder keeps a sealed session and the agents get their own profile.

Will passwords end up in the video?

No. Anything typed into a secret field is stored as redacted and never reaches a prompt, a log, a caption or a frame of footage.

What happens when our UI changes?

Press “Re-run on latest UI”. It films the approved flows again, heals any locators that moved, reports what changed and carries the script, voice and edit over to the new footage.

Can I change the edit myself?

At the preview gate, describe the change in plain words, like “hold longer on the dashboard”. The director revises the edit and checks it again before it renders.
